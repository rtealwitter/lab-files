# Analyst Training Dossier

Use this index while restoring the attachments in the
[Download and Decode lab](https://csci40.rtealwitter.com/topics/03_strings_files/lab.html).
Replace the observation prompts with a few notes as you work.
These notes are practice and receive no points.

The Rickroll is a media-playback test, the communiqué is a historical document
used for encoding practice, and the intercepted message is a fictional case.
The files have been collected for this training exercise.

| Attachment | Role in the dossier | Restoration observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [English communiqué](files/shanghai_communique.english) | Begin with a document whose text is readable in ASCII. | Record the encoding used to open it. |
| [First Chinese copy](files/shanghai_communique.chinese1) | Read the known document using the appropriate Chinese encoding. | Record the encoding that worked. |
| [Second Chinese copy](files/shanghai_communique.chinese2) | Compare another encoding of the same document. | Record the encoding that worked and why a successful decode alone may be misleading. |
| [Intercepted message](part2/secret_message.txt), restored as [secret_message.utf8](part2/secret_message.utf8) | Recover the fictional meeting instructions and save a readable UTF-8 copy. | Record the original encoding and whether the saved copy reopens with readable Portuguese accents and punctuation. |
| [Assigned video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ), saved as [video.mp4](video.mp4) | Confirm that separate picture and sound streams can become one playable file. | Record the saved filename and whether picture and sound play. Update the link if your merged file has a different extension. |

[The DPRK and Unicode](dprk-unicode.md) supplies background for the encoding
history discussed in the lab.
